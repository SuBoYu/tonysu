Developed a question-based recommendation system for retail, combining knowledge
graphs, graph embeddings, and deep reinforcement learning.

Designed a hierarchical action space to improve recommendations with fewer
conversational steps. Trained recommendation models with PyTorch and deployed
the system on Heroku.
